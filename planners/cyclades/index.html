Cyclades travel planners

The Cyclades are the Aegean island group known for whitewashed villages, windmills and sandy beaches. Each planner covers one island, from famous names like Santorini, Mykonos and Naxos to quieter ones such as Folegandros, Serifos and Amorgos.

Every planner is a printable PDF plus an interactive tablet PDF for GoodNotes, with day-by-day itineraries, scan-to-map QR codes, and pages for notes, budget and packing. Checkout is on Etsy and the files download instantly.

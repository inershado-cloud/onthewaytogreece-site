Dodecanese travel planners

The Dodecanese are the island group in the south-east Aegean, close to the Turkish coast. They range from large islands such as Rhodes and Kos to small, quiet ones such as Tilos, Nisyros and Kasos.

Every planner is a printable PDF plus an interactive tablet PDF for GoodNotes, with day-by-day itineraries, scan-to-map QR codes, and pages for notes, budget and packing. Checkout is on Etsy and the files download instantly.

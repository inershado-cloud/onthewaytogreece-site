Peloponnese travel planners

The Peloponnese is the large peninsula south-west of Athens. The planners cover Nafplio, Ancient Corinth and Nemea, Ancient Olympia, Kalamata and Messenia, Mani and Monemvasia, Kalavryta and mountain Arkadia.

Every planner is a printable PDF plus an interactive tablet PDF for GoodNotes, with day-by-day itineraries, scan-to-map QR codes, and pages for notes, budget and packing. Checkout is on Etsy and the files download instantly.

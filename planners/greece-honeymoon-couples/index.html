For two

Greece honeymoon and couples planner

A 15-page planner for a honeymoon or a couples trip, in a printable PDF and an interactive tablet PDF for GoodNotes. It compares five islands, Santorini, Milos, Naxos, Paros and Hydra, so you can choose together and keep one shared plan.

What is inside

  • An extendable 3 to 5 day plan for two: Santorini, Milos, Naxos, Paros and Hydra, one island a day
  • A neutral comparison of the five islands, to help you choose your route together
  • Getting around as a couple: ferries, domestic flights from Athens, transfers, and car and scooter notes
  • Eight dinners for two, each with a QR code that opens the place in Google Maps
  • 10 things to do as a couple, a bucket list, and a packing list for two
  • A budget for two with a daily expense log, a taverna log, and an "Our story" memories page

Printable and tablet editions

You get two PDFs of 15 pages each: a printable edition for A4 or US Letter, and an interactive edition for GoodNotes and other tablet apps, with 11 clickable side tabs. Ferry timetables, flights and restaurant hours change by season, so check them close to your travel dates.

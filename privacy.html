Privacy

This website is a simple information page. It has no accounts, no shop checkout, no cookies and no advertising trackers. We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service that sets no cookies and does not track you across sites, to count page visits. If you use the "New planners and sale alerts" box, your email app opens with a message to info@onthewaytogreece.gr; we only use your address to tell you about new planners and you can ask us to remove it at any time.

Purchases are made on Etsy, which has its own privacy policy. When you follow a link to Etsy, Instagram or TikTok, those services apply their own rules.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record technical data such as your IP address in its server logs, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Product images are loaded from Etsy's servers.

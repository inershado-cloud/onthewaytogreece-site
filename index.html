You receive a complete PDF package containing two editions: (1) an interactive tablet PDF with clickable navigation tabs made for GoodNotes, Notability, and Noteshelf, and (2) a high-resolution printable PDF formatted for standard A4 / US Letter home printers.
Every destination highlight, restaurant, and viewpoint includes a direct QR code. If you print the planner, scan it with your smartphone camera. If you use the digital tablet edition, simply tap the QR code or link to launch Google Maps with the exact pin and directions.

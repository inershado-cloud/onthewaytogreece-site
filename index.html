FAQ

Questions

What do I receive?

A PDF planner for one destination: a printable version and an interactive tablet version, available to download from Etsy after payment.

Can I use it on a tablet?

Yes. The tablet PDF is made for GoodNotes and similar apps, with clickable navigation tabs. You can also print the classic PDF.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

These are instant digital downloads, so Etsy does not accept cancellations once paid. If something is wrong with your file, email info@onthewaytogreece.gr or message the shop on Etsy.

Is VAT included?

Checkout happens on Etsy, which shows the final price and applies any taxes it collects for your country before you pay.

My destination is not there. Can you add it?

Message the shop on Etsy or email us. New destinations are added regularly.

Where can I follow along?

On Instagram and TikTok, @onthewaytogreece.

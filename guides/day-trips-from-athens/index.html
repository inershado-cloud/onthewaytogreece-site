Athens & Attica

Day trips from Athens

Five trips that each start and end in central Athens, so you can do all five or pick the ones that suit your trip. Here is how they compare.

The five trips at a glance

TripEach wayHowBest for
Delphi and Arachovaabout 3 h by bus, about 2 h 15 m by carKTEL bus from Liosion, or a hire carMountain sanctuary, Apollo ruins and museum
Hydraabout 1 h 15 m to 2 h by ferryPiraeus Gate E8Car-free stone harbour, hiking and coves
Aegina (and Agistri)about 30 to 40 min fast, 1 h 15 m conventionalPiraeus Gate E8Temple of Aphaia, Perdika and pistachios
Argolid loopabout 2 h 10 m by KTEL to Nafplio; 1.5 to 2 h by car loopCar hire, tour, or KTEL from KifissosCorinth Canal, Mycenae, Epidaurus and Nafplio
Cape Sounionabout 1.5 to 2 h by bus, 1 to 1.5 h by carKTEL Attikis from Pedion Areos, or carCoastal road and the Temple of Poseidon at sunset

Know your terminals

  • Piraeus Gate E8 serves the Saronic ferries to Hydra and Aegina/Agistri. It is a short walk from Piraeus Metro (Lines 1 and 3).
  • KTEL Liosion (Liosion St) serves Central Greece, including Delphi and Arachova.
  • KTEL Kifissos (Kifissou Ave) serves the Peloponnese, including Nafplio.
  • Pedion Areos (Square Aigyptou) is the stop for the KTEL Attikis coastal bus to Cape Sounion.

What to book ahead

  • Archaeological sites: use the official e-ticket site, tickets.hh.gr, and check seasonal hours.
  • Hydra: book a high-speed ferry ticket in peak season.
  • Aegina: check the Temple of Aphaia winter opening days.
  • Argolid: a hire car or a tour makes the loop practical. Bring an International Driving Permit if your licence requires one.

Which to pick

Short on time? Aegina has the shortest crossing, at about 30 to 40 minutes by fast ferry. Want a full day of ancient sites? Combine Corinth, Mycenae, Epidaurus and Nafplio by car. Want to see the mountains? Delphi and Arachova.

Emergency numbers worth saving: 112 (EU emergency), 100 (police), 166 (ambulance), and the multilingual Tourist Police helpline 1571.

Always check current ferry, bus and opening-time schedules before you book: they change with the season.

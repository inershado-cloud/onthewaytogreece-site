Before you go

Greek island packing list

A short list for an island trip, grouped so you can print it or copy it into your phone. Tick off what applies to your trip.

Beach

  • Swimsuits and a beach towel
  • High-SPF sunscreen, hat and sunglasses
  • Water shoes (many coves are pebbly)
  • Snorkel mask
  • Beach bag
  • Book or e-reader

Clothes

  • Light cotton clothes
  • An evening outfit for town
  • A modest cover-up for monasteries and churches
  • A light jacket for sea breezes and boat rides
  • Comfortable sandals, plus sturdy shoes if you plan to hike
  • Sleepwear, underwear and socks

Documents and money

  • ID or passport
  • Ferry and flight tickets
  • Booking confirmations
  • Driving licence (and an International Driving Permit if you need one)
  • Some cash: small tavernas and some sites take cash only
  • Travel insurance details

Health and tech

  • Phone, charger and a power bank
  • Camera
  • Any regular medication and first-aid basics
  • Mosquito repellent and after-sun lotion
  • A reusable water bottle

Island-specific tips

  • Santorini: black sand gets very hot by midday, so wear sandals, and take a hat for the unshaded Fira to Oia path.
  • Naxos: bring sturdy shoes, a hat and plenty of water if you plan to climb Mount Zas.
  • Meteora: long trousers for men and a long skirt for women are expected at the monasteries.
  • Any island in August: book ferries and rooms well ahead.

Always check current ferry, bus and opening-time schedules before you book: they change with the season.
